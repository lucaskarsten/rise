1. What Rise is

Rise is a free desktop companion for League of Legends. It shows suggestions before a game, short reminders during a game and a review after a game, using the player’s own data and aggregate statistics.

2. Use of the app

  • Rise is provided free of charge, as is, without warranties of any kind.
  • Suggestions are informational. The player makes every decision in the game.
  • Use Rise only with your own Riot account and in line with Riot Games’ Terms of Service.
  • Don’t use Rise to cheat, automate gameplay, or harass or identify other players.

3. Riot Games

Rise is an independent project and is not affiliated with Riot Games. Game data comes from Riot’s official APIs and static data. Riot Games may limit or end access to its APIs at any time, which may stop parts of Rise from working.

4. AI-generated content
